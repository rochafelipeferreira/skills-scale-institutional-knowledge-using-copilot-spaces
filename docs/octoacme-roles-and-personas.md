# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Architect / Platform Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define the system direction, major technical decisions, and platform standards that keep delivery consistent, scalable, and maintainable. They work closely with Product, PM, and engineering teams to turn strategy into practical implementation guidance.

### Responsibilities
- Define architecture patterns, technical standards, and integration approach
- Review major design decisions and trade-offs with the delivery team
- Identify platform, scalability, and dependency risks early
- Ensure technical work aligns with business goals and delivery constraints
- Support developers with technical direction and design validation

### Goals
- Maintain a coherent and resilient technical foundation
- Reduce avoidable rework and integration bottlenecks
- Help the team deliver solutions that are scalable and secure

### Typical Communication
- Architecture review meetings and design discussions
- Technical design docs and decision records
- Alignment with PM on milestones, risks, and dependency management

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to ensure technical feasibility and roadmap alignment
- Partners with Project Managers to surface delivery risks and sequencing challenges
- Guides Developers on implementation choices and system boundaries
- Coordinates with QA on testability, observability, and release confidence

---

## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads protect the organization by ensuring projects meet required controls, secure design standards, and compliance obligations. They help teams integrate security practices early rather than as an afterthought.

### Responsibilities
- Review security requirements, risk exposure, and compliance needs
- Define secure design expectations and validation checkpoints
- Support threat modeling, secure coding practices, and vulnerability review
- Escalate critical risks to leadership and affected partners
- Help the team balance business speed with risk mitigation

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across delivery
- Ensure products meet required controls and policies
- Embed security practices into normal delivery workflows

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews, threat assessments, and governance checkpoints
- Escalation notifications for high-risk findings
- Coordination with PM and engineering on remediation owners and timing

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to ensure customer and regulatory needs are reflected in scope
- Collaborates with Project Managers to manage risk timelines and dependencies
- Reviews implementation changes with Developers and QA to confirm controls are validated
- Supports stakeholder transparency during risk or audit-related communications

---

## UX / Design Lead or Researcher

### Role Summary
UX and Design Leads ensure that solutions are usable, accessible, and centered on customer needs. They translate user insights into clear design direction and help the team validate that the product experience delivers value.

### Responsibilities
- Define user flows, interaction patterns, and design standards
- Gather or translate user research into actionable product guidance
- Review usability, accessibility, and experience quality with the team
- Partner with Product and engineering to prioritize experience outcomes
- Validate that implementation matches the intended user experience

### Goals
- Improve customer value and usability
- Reduce friction in key workflows and experiences
- Align product decisions with user needs and business goals

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews, user research sessions, and feedback loops
- Collaboration with Product Managers on prioritization and problem framing
- Cross-functional review with Developers and QA on polish and usability validation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to define customer outcomes and feature priorities
- Supports Project Managers by clarifying experience-related dependencies and timeline impacts
- Helps Developers interpret design intent and acceptance criteria
- Provides QA with usability and accessibility considerations for validation

---

## Release Manager / Delivery Coordinator

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the readiness, timing, and communication needed for successful delivery. They reduce operational risk by ensuring deploy plans, validation, and stakeholder updates are aligned across teams.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release windows, milestones, and deployment sequencing
- Confirm acceptance criteria, test readiness, and rollback plans
- Communicate release status to stakeholders and support teams
- Identify deployment risks and cross-team dependencies early
- Support post-release validation and issue triage

### Goals
- Increase release reliability and predictability
- Minimize avoidable operational disruption
- Ensure stakeholders are informed and prepared during rollout

### Typical Communication
- Release checklists, deployment reviews, and status updates
- Coordination meetings across engineering, QA, PM, and support
- Post-deploy communication and incident follow-up

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Project Managers on timeline and readiness planning
- Coordinates with Developers and QA to validate release quality and rollback readiness
- Works with Product Managers to confirm customer-facing release communication
- Aligns with Support / Customer Success teams to monitor issues after launch

---

## Support / Customer Success Liaison

### Role Summary
Support and Customer Success Liaisons connect delivery teams with real-world user feedback, service issues, and adoption signals. They help ensure product decisions reflect the experience of customers and support teams after launch.

### Responsibilities
- Capture recurring issues, feedback, and escalations from users or support teams
- Share customer-driven insights with Product and delivery teams
- Help prioritize follow-up work based on customer impact and urgency
- Participate in incident or issue triage when customer-facing issues arise
- Support stakeholder communication around service experience and changes

### Goals
- Reduce customer friction and service disruption
- Convert real-world feedback into actionable product improvements
- Strengthen cross-functional awareness of operational reality

### Typical Communication
- Support tickets, customer feedback loops, and weekly issue summaries
- Stakeholder updates on customer impacts and adoption trends
- Coordination with Product, PM, and engineering on follow-up actions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides Product Managers with direct customer insight and prioritization input
- Works with Project Managers to flag risk, dependencies, and support impacts
- Shares critical issues with Developers and QA for troubleshooting and validation
- Helps Release Managers communicate post-deploy impact and operational readiness
